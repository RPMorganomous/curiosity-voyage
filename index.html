Text Messaging Program & Privacy Policy

About this messaging program

Curiosity Voyage, LLC operates a low-volume customer care text messaging program. Through this program, Ember, the personal assistant service of Curiosity Voyage, LLC, sends conversational text messages to individuals who have opted in. The program's sending number is +1 979-942-1232.

How to opt in

An individual opts in by providing their mobile phone number directly to Curiosity Voyage, LLC in person or in writing, and confirming that they wish to receive text messages from Ember at +1 979-942-1232. No other opt-in method is used. Phone numbers are never purchased, rented, or obtained from third parties, and we do not message anyone who has not opted in.

  • Message frequency varies — this is a low-volume program, typically a few messages per week.
  • Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.
  • Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for assistance.
  • Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase or service.
